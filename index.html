What we make

Map Behind History is an independent educational channel. Each video takes one odd fact about the map and explains the history behind it: why a town is split between two countries, why Brazil speaks Portuguese, why Chile is so long and thin, why Greenland is icy and Iceland is green.

We publish short history Shorts and a longer deep-dive every week. Every video lists its sources in the description.
