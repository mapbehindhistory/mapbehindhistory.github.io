Why Did Rome Build a Wall Across Britain?

In AD 122 Emperor Hadrian ordered a wall across northern Britain: 73 miles (80 Roman miles) from Wallsend on the Tyne to Bowness-on-Solway, built by soldiers of three legions in about six years and up to about 15 feet tall. A gated milecastle stood roughly every Roman mile, with two turrets between each pair, plus larger forts.
