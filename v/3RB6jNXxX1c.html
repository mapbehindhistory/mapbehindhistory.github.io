Why Does Brazil Speak Portuguese? The 1494 Line

On 7 June 1494, at Tordesillas in Spain, Castile and Portugal agreed a line from pole to pole, 370 leagues west of the Cape Verde Islands: new lands to the west for Castile, to the east for Portugal. It moved the line set by Pope Alexander VI's 1493 bull, 100 leagues out. Later maps placed the line anywhere from about 42° to 50° W.
