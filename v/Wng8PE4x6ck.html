Why Are Europe and Africa Only 14 km Apart?

At its narrowest the Strait of Gibraltar separates Europe and Africa by about 14 km, and it is the gateway between the Atlantic and the Mediterranean. Around 6 million years ago the connection closed completely and salt deposits formed; about 5.33 million years ago it reopened and has stayed open since.
