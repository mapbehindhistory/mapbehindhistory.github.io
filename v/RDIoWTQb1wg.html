Where Is the Loneliest Spot on Earth?

Point Nemo, the oceanic pole of inaccessibility in the South Pacific, is about 2,688 km from the nearest land: Ducie Island, Motu Nui near Easter Island, and Maher Island off Antarctica. Survey engineer Hrvoje Lukatela first calculated its position in 1992 and named it after Jules Verne's Captain Nemo (Latin for 'nobody').
