Why Is Greenland Icy and Iceland Green?

Greenland is about 80% ice and Iceland is mostly green, so why do their names sound swapped? The names came from two Vikings a century apart: one bad winter in Iceland and one sales pitch in Greenland. The real reason one island is frozen is written in the ocean: the warm North Atlantic and Irminger currents on one side, the Arctic East Greenland Current on the other.
