Privacy Policy

Map Behind History · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This privacy policy covers the Map Behind History website (this site) and the Map Behind History Cross-poster, a private tool the channel's owner uses to publish the channel's own videos to its own social media accounts.

1. Who we are

Map Behind History is an independent educational video channel, published on YouTube, Instagram and Pinterest as @mapbehindhistory. Contact: creativefella92@gmail.com.

2. This website

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. We do not collect any personal information from visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

3. The Cross-poster tool

The Cross-poster connects only to the channel's own accounts through the official Instagram API and Pinterest API, with the owner's permission. It is used to:

  • publish the channel's own videos as Instagram Reels and Pinterest Pins, with a title, description and link back to YouTube;
  • read basic statistics (such as views and saves) for those posts, and the list of the channel's own Pinterest boards.

The tool does not access, collect or store information about any other person's account, followers, messages or content.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and post statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server we run, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

5. Your choices and deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time in Instagram (Settings › Apps and websites) or Pinterest (Settings › Apps). To ask about or request deletion of any stored data, email creativefella92@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

6. Children

This site and tool are not directed at children and collect no information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
